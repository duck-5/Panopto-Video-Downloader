# Privacy Policy

The Panopto Video Downloader extension respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

## Data Collection and Usage
This extension **does not collect, store, or transmit any user data**. 

* **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, or any other personal identifiers.
* **No Browsing History**: We do not track the websites you visit or your browsing habits. The extension only interacts with the Panopto video page when you explicitly click the extension icon (using the `activeTab` permission).
* **No Analytics**: We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools.
* **Local Processing**: All logic required to extract video links and facilitate downloads happens locally within your browser. No data is sent to external servers or the developer.

## Permissions
The extension requests the following permissions for functional reasons only:
* `activeTab`: To read the video title and stream URL on the specific Panopto page you are viewing when you trigger the extension.
* `scripting`: To safely extract video metadata from the page.
* `downloads`: To trigger the browser's built-in download manager for the video file.

## Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this file within the source code repository.
